Yes, I said water trains. Laugh if you must, detective, but this is a deadly serious business. It’s been a bumpy road, but even a blind man can see that Preston Harcourt is a visionary genius. The first trans-Pacific railway will connect Seattle to Honolulu to Tokyo by this time next year and make investors filthy rich. But Mr. Harcourt has made many enemies along the way. Now he’s disappeared somewhere in these sea sectors, and we fear a jealous rival has murdered him. We’ve launched a number of search buoys. Here’s what we can tell you:

  • Every cell must be either shaded or unshaded. A few known shaded cells have been filled in for you.
  • Cells containing search buoys are never shaded.
  • Each search buoy has reported back a positive integer which is the number of unshaded cells it can see in the four cardinal directions, including its own cell. Buoys can see beyond other buoys but not beyond shaded cells or the edges of the map.
  • Communications are garbled from some buoys, represented by question marks. We know these cells are unshaded, but we can’t tell how many unshaded cells these buoys can see.
  • Buoys that are underlined have their views blocked in exactly 3 cardinal directions by shaded cells and/or map edges.
  • Buoys that are not underlined do not have their views blocked in exactly 3 cardinal directions.

The board of directors and angel investors are quite nervous. Please find out what’s happened to Mr. Harcourt!
