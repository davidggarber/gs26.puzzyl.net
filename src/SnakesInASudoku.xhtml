Below is a Sudoku-like grid. The rules are:

  • The distribution of digits is pyramidal.
    There are eight 1s, seven 2s, six 3s, five 4s, four 5s, three 6s, two 7s, and a single 8.
  • Eachrow and hexagonal diagonalcontains only unique digits – no duplicates.
    However, the middle black hexagon is a barrier. Duplicates can exist on either side of that barrier.
  • A snake is any sequence of adjacent and consecutively numbered cells starting at 1. In this puzzle,
    an optimal snake grouping creates seven snakes: one of length 8, three of length 5, and three of
    length 2. The tail numbers of each of these snakes have been provided. For the four longest
    snakes, all member cells for a given snake share the same color and underline.
